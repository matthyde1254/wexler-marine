About the shop

Wexler Marine LLC is owned and operated by a single Yamaha certified technician. There's no dispatch desk and no rotating crew — the person who quotes the job is the person who does the work.

All service is mobile — work is done at your dock, marina, or storage location, scheduled around your availability.
